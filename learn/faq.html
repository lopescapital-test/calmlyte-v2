About Calmlyte

What is Calmlyte?
Calmlyte is a green-light wellness lighting brand. Its products create a soft, forest-inspired green-light environment in the spaces you live and work in, with four built-in settings for different times of day.
What is green light therapy?
Green light therapy uses a specific narrow band of green light (around 520–530 nm) to help manage chronic pain, reduce migraine intensity, improve sleep, and improve the appearance of uneven skin tone.
Is Calmlyte a medical device?
No. Calmlyte is a general wellness product, not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Always follow the user manual and discontinue use if discomfort occurs.
What makes Calmlyte different from other lights?
Calmlyte is built specifically around a narrow-band green spectrum and a forest-inspired aesthetic, with settings designed to change the feel of a room through the day rather than simply lighting it.

The light & the settings

What light spectrum does Calmlyte use?
Calmlyte is built around a narrow-band 520–530 nm green spectrum, with 490–500 nm cyan-green and 590 nm amber support depending on the product and the setting.
What are the four settings?
Haven is a balanced everyday setting; Focus is a brighter daytime setting; Stillness is a soft, diffused setting for quiet rooms; and Dusk is a warm, low-intensity evening setting.
How long is a typical session?
Sessions typically range from about 10 to 45 minutes depending on the setting. Start with lower brightness and shorter sessions, then adjust based on comfort and preference.
Can I control brightness and settings?
Yes. Calmlyte products offer adjustable brightness, and most connect by Bluetooth app with the four built-in settings. [FACTS: confirm which SKUs have app control vs. remote-only]
Do I need to wear protective glasses?
Do not stare directly into the LEDs on any Calmlyte product. The Panel includes protective glasses — use them when using it at close range or higher brightness. Not every Calmlyte product ships with eyewear. Individuals with photosensitivity, eye conditions, epilepsy, or those taking photosensitizing medications should consult a qualified healthcare professional before use.

Choosing a product

Which product should I start with?
Calmlyte comes in four forms. The Panel is a green-forward panel for desks and side tables, and the only product that carries all four settings. The Handheld is portable green light for desks and travel, built for short daytime resets on Focus and Haven. The Belt is a wearable green-light wrap for decompression, worn during quiet hours on Stillness and Haven. The Mask is a personal green-and-amber evening light for close-range use during wind-down and reading, on Dusk and Stillness. Start with the Panel if you want to change a room, and with a wearable or the Handheld if you want the light with you.
What comes in the box?
Contents vary by product. The Panel ships with the panel, an adjustable stand, a power cable, protective glasses, a remote control, and a user manual. The Mask does not include protective glasses.
