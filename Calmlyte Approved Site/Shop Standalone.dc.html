Calmlyte is inspired by shinrin yoku, the Japanese practice of bathing in the forest atmosphere.

It uses a narrow band of visible green light, around 520 to 530 nm — the middle of the visible spectrum, between blue and yellow.

Research is still developing. Early studies suggest this range may:

  • improve sleep
  • manage chronic pain
  • reduce migraine intensity
  • soften surface-level skin discoloration
  • support relaxation and internal regulation
